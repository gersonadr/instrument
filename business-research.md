# Business Research: Ignition (ignitionapp.com)

## 1. Company Overview

**Ignition** (formerly Practice Ignition) was founded in 2013 by **Guy Pearson** (CEO) and **Dane Thomas** (CPO/Co-founder) in Australia. Pearson, an accounting entrepreneur frustrated by antiquated manual processes and chronic late payments from clients, teamed with product designer Thomas to build a platform that transforms how professional services firms engage clients and get paid.

- **Type:** Private company (VC-backed)
- **Founded:** 2013
- **Headquarters:** Sydney, Australia (with offices in San Francisco, London, and other markets)
- **Employees:** ~180
- **Customers:** 8,000–8,500+ firms and agencies (2025)
- **Revenue under management:** $9B+ in total client revenue managed to date; $3.1B processed in 2025 alone
- **Website:** [ignitionapp.com](https://www.ignitionapp.com)

---

## 2. Product Overview

Ignition is the **world's first client engagement and commerce platform** for professional services businesses — an all-in-one solution for proposals, contracts, billing, and automated payment collection. The platform's positioning has evolved from pure proposal software to a full revenue-automation platform: **price, sell, bill, and get paid** in one unified workflow.

### Core Value Proposition
Ignition eliminates the administrative burden of manual invoicing, late-payment chasing, scope creep, and disjointed client onboarding by automating the entire client commercial lifecycle from first contact to recurring collection.

### Key Platform Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Online Proposals** | Branded, digital proposals with embedded services, pricing options, and upsell add-ons |
| **eSignatures** | Built-in e-signature (up to 10 per proposal); no third-party tool needed |
| **Automated Billing** | Recurring billing, upfront payments, one-off invoices triggered automatically on acceptance |
| **Payment Collection** | Stripe-powered credit/debit card and ACH collection; 91% of payments collected automatically in 2025 |
| **AutoCollect** | Import unpaid invoices from QuickBooks Online or Xero; collect outstanding balances via Ignition |
| **Deals (Sales Pipeline)** | CRM-style pipeline — leads → proposals → agreements → payments, all in one view |
| **Forms (Lead Capture)** | Branded intake/contact forms that automatically convert to Deals and then to proposals |
| **Billing Hub** | Consolidated view of all upcoming manual billings across clients |
| **AI Price Insights** | Benchmarks and AI-powered pricing suggestions based on real Ignition platform data |
| **Bulk Price Updates** | Adjust pricing across multiple client agreements in bulk for renewal season |
| **Reporting** | Accounts receivable performance, invoice tracking, and cash flow reporting |
| **Client Portal** | Self-service portal for clients to view, sign, and manage agreements |

## 4. Business Model and Pricing

### Revenue Streams
1. **SaaS Subscription** — tiered monthly/annual plans per user count
2. **Payment Transaction Fees** — percentage-based fee on payments processed through Ignition (Stripe-powered)
3. **Add-on Subscriptions** — AI Price Insights available as an annual add-on (Australia and US markets)

### Pricing Structure (effective May 2025)

| Plan | Price | Users | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | $39/month (billed annually) | 1 | Solopreneurs (<$150K revenue) |
| **Core** | $99/month | Up to 3 | Small firms |
| **Pro** | $229/month | Up to 15 | Growing firms |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Large organizations |

- **14-day free trial** with full feature access; up to $10,000 in payments collected fee-free during trial
- Annual billing offers a discount vs. monthly

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

| Competitor | Positioning | Ignition Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Proposify** | Proposal software; design-forward | Ignition adds billing + payments post-signature; Proposify stops at the signature |
| **PandaDoc** | Document automation, e-signatures, proposals | Ignition has deeper accounting integrations and automated recurring billing |
| **HoneyBook** | Client experience platform for creatives | Ignition stronger in accounting/finance vertical; more automation depth |
| **Better Proposals** | Proposal creation tool | Ignition broader scope (billing, payments, pipeline) |
| **Qwilr** | Proposal + interactive web pages | Ignition stronger on collections and accounting software ecosystem |
| **GoProposal** | Pricing & proposal tool for accountants | Ignition more comprehensive (billing + payments + pipeline + integrations) |
| **Karbon** | Practice management for accountants | Complementary (Ignition + Karbon integration launched 2025); Karbon stronger on workflow/tasks |
| **Sage Accounting, Xero, QBO** | Accounting software with proposal/invoicing | Ignition specializes in the pre-billing engagement layer; integrates with these rather than competing |

### Ignition's Differentiation
- **End-to-end revenue automation:** Uniquely bridges the gap between the proposal signature and the payment collection — competitors typically handle one side or the other
- **Automatic payment on acceptance:** Payment details captured upfront; billing triggers automatically when client signs
- **Accounting-native ecosystem:** Deepest native integrations with accounting-specific tools (CCH Axcess, Thomson Reuters Onvio, ProConnect, Financial Cents, Karbon)
- **AI pricing intelligence:** Benchmarks drawn from real anonymized Ignition platform data across 8,000+ firms

### Ignition's Limitations
- **Stripe-only payments:** No native support for other payment processors (PayPal, Square, etc.)
- **Pricing can escalate:** Add-on costs reduce competitiveness for smaller engagements
- **Primarily serves Anglophone markets:** Strongest in US, Australia, UK, Canada, NZ
- **Not a full practice management tool:** Workflow/task management requires integration with Karbon or Financial Cents

## Summary Assessment

Ignition occupies a differentiated and defensible niche at the intersection of proposal software, contract management, and fintech — specifically for professional services firms that need to automate the commercial client lifecycle end-to-end. While proposal tools like PandaDoc or Proposify stop at the signature, Ignition's unique value is that the signature **triggers billing and payment collection automatically**, dramatically reducing accounts receivable friction and late payments.

The platform has demonstrated strong growth ($9.7M → $27M → $30M → $46.7M in ARR from 2021–2024) with a capital-efficient model. Its 2025 product expansion into pipeline management (Deals), lead capture (Forms), and AutoCollect substantially broadens the total addressable use case beyond proposals alone toward a full revenue operations platform for accounting and professional services firms.

The accounting-native integration ecosystem (CCH Axcess, Thomson Reuters Onvio, ProConnect, Karbon, Financial Cents, Xero, QuickBooks) creates meaningful switching costs and positions Ignition as a platform layer that complements — rather than competes with — the core accounting and practice management software its customers already rely on.

With 8,500+ customers, $3.1B in annual payment volume, and a clear 2026 roadmap toward deeper billing and payment automation, Ignition is well-positioned to expand within its existing customer base and continue penetrating the large global market of accounting and professional services firms seeking to modernize client engagement.
